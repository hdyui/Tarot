The Cards

Symbols, keywords and reference interpretations. Demo set of six cards.

The Star

Hope · Renewal · Calm

A gentle card of healing and open-hearted direction. It invites you to notice what restores you and where quiet hope is already growing.

The Moon

Intuition · Dreams · Unknown

The Moon asks you to move slowly and trust your inner signals. Not everything is clear yet, and that can be a space to explore feelings, not a cause for worry.

The Sun

Warmth · Clarity · Joy

A bright, honest card. It points to what feels energising and simple, and invites you to consider what you would do with a little more confidence.

The Hermit

Solitude · Insight · Guidance

The Hermit suggests stepping back to look inward. A quiet evening, a notebook or a calm conversation may help your own answers surface.

Wheel of Fortune

Cycles · Change · Timing

Life moves in cycles. This card invites you to reflect on what is shifting and which parts of the turn are within your reach.

The Empress

Nurture · Creativity · Abundance

A card of care and creative growth. Consider what deserves tending right now — in your projects, relationships or in yourself.
