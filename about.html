Reading Philosophy

Tarot as a Tool for Reflection

Each reading is a conversation, not a fixed prediction. The cards offer symbols and prompts that can help you consider a question from another perspective.

What to Expect

A calm, unhurried session

  • No prior Tarot experience is needed.
  • You may choose a specific topic or a general question.
  • The reader explains the meaning of each card and invites you to reflect for yourself.
  • You can skip any question you would rather not share.
