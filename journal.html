Notes on Tarot

Short guides to help you feel at ease before your reading.

01

Tarot for Beginners

Explains the structure of the deck and how to read the content of this website.

Read the note

A Tarot deck has 78 cards: 22 Major Arcana that mark big themes, and 56 Minor Arcana in four suits that describe everyday moments. You do not need to memorise anything — read each card as a symbol and notice what it stirs in you.

02

How a Three-Card Spread Works

Introduces the three positions and how to interpret them.

Read the note

ASTRA’s spread uses three positions: The Present, The Hidden and The Possibility. Read them as a short story — where you are, what is beneath the surface, and a perspective you could explore next.

03

Preparing for a Reading

Helps you prepare your question.

Read the note

Choose something open-ended: “What should I pay attention to about…?” works better than yes/no questions. If you would rather not have a question at all, that is perfectly fine.

04

Tarot, Reflection and Personal Choice

Makes clear that Tarot does not replace decisions or professional advice.

Read the note

Tarot can help you slow down and consider options, but the decisions remain yours. It is not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.
